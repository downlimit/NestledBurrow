# Task #048 — Сортировка кровати и звуки огорода

## Goal

Keep the sleeping player deterministically above the selected bed visual and add distinct procedural effects for successful hoe use, planting, and crop destruction caused by a thrown wood or stone resource.

## Acceptance

- the sleeping pose is layered from the actual selected bed visual depth;
- bed collider edits do not control the occupant layer;
- failed farming actions remain silent;
- hoe use, planting and dropped-resource crop impact use distinct procedural definitions;
- no binary assets are added or modified.
